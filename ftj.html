Fuck This Job

C++
Unreal Engine
3D
Group Project
Graduation Project
View on Steam ↗

In Fuck This Job, the player takes control of Jake, a janitor who has been fired after his company decided to replace its employees with AI-powered machines.

Armed with a powerful vacuum cleaner, Jake sets out on a destructive rampage through the company. Vacuum, shoot and kick everything in your path while fighting the machines that replaced human workers.

Developed as a fifth-year graduation project at ARTFX, Fuck This Job is a fast-paced 3D action game combining destruction, combat and environmental interactions with a humorous take on automation and artificial intelligence.

Development Challenges

  • Developing responsive gameplay mechanics combining vacuum interactions, shooting and melee attacks.
  • Creating interactive environments that support fast-paced destruction.
  • Implementing gameplay systems that reward players for chaining destructive actions.
  • Collaborating across programming, design, art, animation and audio disciplines during a large-scale graduation project.

What you'll experience

  • Take revenge on a company that replaced its employees with AI-powered machines.
  • Vacuum, shoot and kick your way through interactive environments.
  • Destroy robotic machines and everything standing in your path.
  • Chain destruction combos to maximize your performance and paychecks.
  • Discover and collect productivity figures throughout your destructive journey.
